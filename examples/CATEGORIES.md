# Categories

One category per line; indent to make sub-categories; text after the colon describes what the photos look like.

- Coral: hard coral colonies on the reef
  - Bleaching: pale or bright white coral
  - Disease: coral with dark bands or patches of dead tissue
- Sponge: sponges on the reef or sponge fragments on ropes
- Diver: a scuba diver working underwater
- Fish: fish, turtles, rays and other sea life
- People: people on land, at events, talks or in the lab
- Landscape: beaches, the sea or the coast with no main person

## Notes

- This is an example. In the app, categories are edited on the Categories page and saved as CATEGORIES.md inside your photo folder.
