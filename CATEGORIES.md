# Categories

One category per line; indent to make sub-categories; text after the colon describes what the photos look like.

- Photos - Coral: hard corals and coral work
  - Outplants: corals placed by the team on plugs, discs, nails or epoxy, often with tags
    - APAL: Acropora palmata (elkhorn coral), thick flat branches like moose antlers
    - ACER: Acropora cervicornis (staghorn coral), thin cylindrical branches like deer antlers
    - CNAT: Colpophyllia natans (boulder brain coral), large dome with wide meandering valleys
    - PCLI: Pseudodiploria clivosa (knobby brain coral), encrusting brain coral with knobby ridges
    - OFAV: Orbicella faveolata (mountainous star coral), lumpy mounds covered in small star-shaped polyps
    - PSTR: Pseudodiploria strigosa (symmetrical brain coral), rounded brain coral with even narrow grooves
    - ODIF: Oculina diffusa (diffuse ivory bush coral), small bushy branches with pale tips
    - MLAM: Mycetophyllia lamarckiana (ridged cactus coral), flat plates with radiating ridges
    - DLAB: Diploria labyrinthiformis (grooved brain coral), brain coral with deep grooves along its ridges
    - MMEA: Meandrina meandrites (maze coral), maze-like valleys separated by sharp ridges
    - MCAV: Montastraea cavernosa (great star coral), boulder with large round polyps
    - SBOU: Solenastrea bournoni (smooth star coral), smooth rounded colony with tiny polyps
  - Natural: wild coral colonies growing on the reef, not placed by the team
    - APAL: Acropora palmata (elkhorn coral), thick flat branches like moose antlers
    - MALI: Mycetophyllia aliciae (knobby cactus coral), flat plates with knobby ridges
    - OFAV: Orbicella faveolata (mountainous star coral), lumpy mounds covered in small star-shaped polyps
    - OFRA: Orbicella franksi (boulder star coral), irregular boulders with bumpy polyps
    - DLAB: Diploria labyrinthiformis (grooved brain coral), brain coral with deep grooves along its ridges
    - MCAV: Montastraea cavernosa (great star coral), boulder with large round polyps
    - PFUR: Porites furcata (branched finger coral), thin forked finger-like branches
    - PAST: Porites astreoides (mustard hill coral), yellow-green lumpy mound
    - SBOU: Solenastrea bournoni (smooth star coral), smooth rounded colony with tiny polyps
    - ALAM: Agaricia lamarcki (Lamarck's sheet coral), thin overlapping plates or shelves
    - MAUR: Madracis auretenra (yellow pencil coral), thin yellow pencil-like branches
    - PPOR: Porites porites (finger coral), thick blunt finger-like branches
  - Bleaching: corals turning pale or bright white
  - Disease: coral with lesions, dark bands or patches of dead tissue
  - Spawning: corals releasing eggs or sperm bundles at night
  - Soft Coral: sea fans, sea rods and other soft swaying corals
  - Predation: fish, snails or worms eating coral, bite marks on coral
- Photos - Sponges: sponges and sponge restoration work
  - Sponge Nursery: sponge fragments growing on ropes or frames underwater
  - Sponge Land-Based Nursery: sponges growing in tanks or raceways on land
  - Sponge Outplanting: divers attaching sponge fragments to the reef
  - Sponge Outplants: sponge fragments fixed to the reef with epoxy or ties
  - Sponge Monitoring: divers measuring or photographing sponges with scale bars or quadrats
  - Sponge Maintenance: divers cleaning nursery ropes or frames
  - Sponge Surveys: divers with clipboards and transect tapes surveying sponges
  - Sponge ID Slates: underwater slates or labels with sponge identification codes
  - Natural Sponges: wild sponges on the reef, barrel, tube or rope sponges
  - Sponge Disease/Bleaching: pale, rotting or patchy sponges
  - Sponge Outreach: people learning about sponges on land, talks or events
- Photos - Fragging: people at a table or in a lab cutting coral into small fragments, tools and trays
- Photos - Staff: posed portrait or headshot of a team member, often in an I.CARE shirt
- Intern Portraits: posed portrait of a young person outdoors or in greenery
- Donors: group holding a big donation check, donor events, sponsor handoffs
- Kylie and Mike: photos of Kylie and Mike together
- Online Presentations: screenshots of video calls, slides or webinars
- PADI Women Divers Day: group of women divers at a dive event, PADI banners
- Photos - Marine Organisms: fish, turtles, rays, eels and invertebrates on the reef
- Photos - Outplanting/M...: divers planting or monitoring corals on the reef
- Photos - Science/Research: lab work, microscopes, data sheets and scientific equipment
- Landscape Imagery: scenic land, sea or beach views with no main person
- Outreach: talks, school visits, booths and community events on land
- Halloween Party: people in costumes, Halloween decorations
- Trash Derby: beach or ocean cleanup, collected trash, bags of debris
- Content: social-media style shots, graphics and promotional images
- Videos - Coral: video focused on corals or the reef
- Videos - Sponge: video focused on sponges
- Videos - Marine Organisms: video of fish, turtles and other sea life
- Videos - Outplanting/M...: video of divers planting or monitoring corals
- Videos - Science/Research: video of lab or field research
