# Categories

Each `##` heading is a category name (used as the folder / album name).
The paragraph under it describes what belongs there. Everything before the
first `##` is ignored, so you can keep notes here.

## Fragging
People at a table or lab fragmenting coral, coral frags, tools, data sheets.

## Staff
Posed portrait or headshot of a team member, often in an I.CARE shirt.

## Landscape
Scenic land, sea or beach views with no main person.

## Underwater
Underwater scenes, divers, reef work.

# Videos

Optional. Categories under a `# Videos` heading are used for videos only.
If this section is missing, videos use the same categories as photos.

## Videos - Coral
Video focused on corals or the reef.

## Videos - Research
Video of lab or field research activity.
